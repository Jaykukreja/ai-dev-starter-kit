Something is broken: $ARGUMENTS

Follow the scope rules in AGENTS.md. Do not go hunting through the whole
codebase.

1. **Translate the error.** What is it actually saying, in plain English.

2. **Your best guess at the cause**, and how confident you are. Say "I am
   guessing" when you are guessing.

3. **One thing to check first**, and the exact way to check it. Not a list of
   five things — the single most likely one.

If I have not given you the actual error text, ask for it before guessing. If I
have not said what I expected to happen versus what did happen, ask for that
too.

Do not fix it. Do not edit any files. Walk me to it so I find it myself — that
is the point.

If I come back and say your guess was wrong, take that seriously as
information. Say what it rules out, then give me the next thing to check.
